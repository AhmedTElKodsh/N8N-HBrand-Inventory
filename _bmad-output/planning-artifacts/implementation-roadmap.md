# Proposed implementation roadmap

Baseline: 2026-10-27. This is an ordered backlog strategy, not an approved schedule or evidence of completed implementation. The [epics](epics.md) own story acceptance criteria.

| Stage | Outcome | Evidence before advancing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 — Documentation baseline | Discussion, contract, planning and questions captured | Documentation checks and preserved unresolved inputs; no runtime success inferred. |
| 1 — Runtime/access and source audit | Local engine selected; scoped source manifest, original rows/images and duplicate investigation | Own configured credentials; full scope accounting; reliable read-only inventory existence lookup. |
| 2 — Family identity and bilingual content | Source-confirmed family, stable SKU, observed variants and fact-grounded English/Arabic copy | Distinct-print counterexamples stay separate; unresolved size/price/weight facts remain visible. |
| 3 — Photography pilot | Comparable provider attempts and owner-corrected initial gallery | Actual downloaded files, retained failures, product-fidelity review and scoped acceptance. |
| 4 — Workbook enrichment | Preserved staged copy, guarded same-file update and read-back | Drawing/formula/row alignment comparisons; concurrency mechanism established. |
| 5 — Inventory pilot | One exact product/variant payload saved and freshly verified | Real controls/lookup/warehouse/price semantics known; duplicate and unknown-save recovery passes. |
| 6 — Wider operation | Representative products and failures covered; bounded unattended policy selected | Recorded acceptance results, measured batch/budget limits, later review policy and operational recovery evidence. |
